# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want working on hypruse browser to be safe and respectful for everyone. Be considerate, disagree
about code without attacking people, and give others room to ask questions or change their minds.

Welcome behaviour includes:

- specific, useful feedback on code and documentation;
- respect for people of every background, identity, ability and level of experience;
- owning mistakes and repairing harm where you can;
- protecting other people's browsing data and private information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, publishing someone's
private information and sustained disruption of project spaces are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break
this code of conduct. They may restrict someone's participation, for a time or for good, when
their behaviour puts people or the project at risk.

Report a concern privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not use a public issue for
anything sensitive. Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy as far as they can, look at
the context, and explain the outcome to the people involved when it is safe to do so.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and whenever someone represents the project in public.

It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
